3 LONG TITLE

4 General Description:

5   This bill supplements or reduces appropriations otherwise provided for the support and

6 operation of state government for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2024 and ending June 30,

7 2025.

8 Highlighted Provisions:

9   This bill:

10  ▸ provides appropriations for the use and support of higher education and certain state

11 agencies;

12  ▸ authorizes full time employment levels for certain internal service funds;

13  ▸ provides appropriations for other purposes as described; and

14  ▸ provides intent language.

15 Money Appropriated in this Bill:

16  This bill appropriates $1,223,859,700 in operating and capital budgets for fiscal year 2025,

17 including:

18  ▸ $15,974,900 from General Fund; and

19  ▸ ($260,100) from Income Tax Fund; and

20  ▸ $1,208,144,900 from various sources as detailed in this bill.

21  This bill appropriates ($21,438,200) in expendable funds and accounts for fiscal year 2025, all

22 of which is from the various sources as detailed in this bill.

23  This bill appropriates $31,056,100 in business-like activities for fiscal year 2025, including:

24  ▸ $1,750,000 from General Fund; and

25  ▸ $260,100 from Income Tax Fund; and

26  ▸ $29,046,000 from various sources as detailed in this bill.

27  This bill appropriates ($6,893,200) in restricted fund and account transfers for fiscal year 2025,

29  ▸ ($4,728,500) from General Fund; and

32  This bill appropriates $48,488,000 in transfers to unrestricted funds for fiscal year 2025, all of
