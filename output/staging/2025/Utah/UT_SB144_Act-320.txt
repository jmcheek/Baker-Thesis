3 LONG TITLE

4 General Description:

5   This bill addresses sexual crimes.

6 Highlighted Provisions:

7   This bill:

8   ▸ provides a definition of a female breast for certain sexual and sexual exploitation offenses

9 that includes a breast that is undeveloped or partially developed;

10  ▸ expands the definition of "child sexual abuse material" to include a minor observing or

11 being used for sexually explicit conduct;

12  ▸ expands the definition of "sexually explicit conduct" concerning certain bodily functions

13 for certain offenses;

14  ▸ changes the mental state necessary to commit the offense of sexual exploitation of a

15 minor;

16  ▸ expands the offense of sexual exploitation of a minor to include when an actor accesses

17 child sexual abuse material with the intent to view;

18  ▸ provides a safe harbor provision for certain employees and independent contractors who

19 are required to access or view child sexual abuse material within the scope of

20 employment;

21  ▸ changes the mental state necessary to commit the offense of aggravated sexual

22 exploitation of a minor;

23  ▸ amends the offense of aggravated sexual exploitation of a minor to remove a lesser

24 penalty for circumstances when the child sexual abuse material depicts an individual

25 who is 14 years old or older or pubescent; and

26  ▸ makes technical and conforming changes.
