1 Enrolled, An Act,

2

3   Relating to the Alabama Science, Technology,

4 Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) Council; to create the

5 council within the Department of Workforce; to provide for the

6 membership of the council, including the executive committee

7 of the council and additional members appointed by the

8 Governor and Secretary of Workforce; to provide for meetings;

9 and to provide for the duties and functions of the council.

10 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA:

11  Section 1. For the purposes of this act, the following

12 terms have the following meanings:

13  (1) COUNCIL or STEM COUNCIL. The Alabama Science,

14 Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) Council

15 created by this act.

16  (2) DEPARTMENT. The Department of Workforce.

17  (3) EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE. The executive committee of the

18 council.

19  (4) STEM. Science, technology, engineering, and math.

20  (5) STEM CAREERS. Careers that utilize concepts from

21 the STEM fields including, but not limited to, engineering,

22 life science, accounting, astronomy, physics, chemistry, earth

23 science, math and computing, information science, artificial

24 intelligence, health and medicine, including physicians,

25 nurses, technicians, and social sciences, including political

26 science and economics.

27  (6) STEM EDUCATION. Education that entails more than

28 achieving competency in individual STEM subject areas and

                           Page 1
               HB365 Enrolled

29 requires students to be able to integrate concepts from

30 multiple disciplines and use that knowledge to solve complex

31 problems. The term often includes instructional practices that

32 actively engage all students in an interdisciplinary project

33 or problem-based approach, tied to a real world, authentic

34 challenge through which students use various technologies to

35 assist their inquiry, research, and communication.

36  Section 2. (a) The Alabama Science, Technology,

37 Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) Council is established as

38 a state entity within the department to advise the Governor,

39 Lieutenant Governor, Secretary of Workforce, Executive

40 Director of the Alabama Commission on Higher Education,

41 Chancellor of the Alabama Community College System, State

42 Superintendent of Education, Chair of the Alabama Workforce

43 Board, and the Legislature on ways to improve STEM-related

44 education, career awareness, and workforce development across

45 the state.

46  (b) The Chief of the Workforce Pathways Division of the

47 department shall be responsible for the administrative

48 functions of the council and, upon recommendation of the

49 Executive Committee of the STEM Council, shall hire an

50 individual employed by the department to serve as the Director

51 of the STEM Council. The director shall serve in that position

52 at the pleasure of the Executive Committee of the STEM

53 Council.

54  Section 3. (a) The membership of the STEM Council shall

55 consist of all of the following:

56  (1) Two members appointed by the Governor with

               Page 2
                      HB365 Enrolled

57 expertise in STEM education or workforce development.

58  (2) Two members appointed by the Secretary of Workforce

59 with expertise in workforce development.

60  (3) The following public officials or their designees:

61  a. The Chair of the Alabama Workforce Board.

62  b. The Secretary of Commerce.

63  c. The Secretary of Early Childhood Education.

64  d. The State Superintendent of Education.

65  e. The Chancellor of the Alabama Community College

66 System.

67  f. The Executive Director of the Alabama Commission on

68 Higher Education.

69  (4) One member appointed by the Governor from each of

70 the state's workforce regions.

71  (5) Additional members appointed by the Governor and

72 Secretary of Workforce as representatives of the state's STEM

73 stakeholders, including STEM industry representatives, formal

74 STEM education organizations, and informal STEM education

75 organizations.

76  (b) The appointing authorities shall coordinate their

77 appointments to assure the council membership is inclusive and

78 reflects the racial, gender, geographic, urban, rural, and

79 economic diversity of the state.

80  (c) STEM Council members shall serve until their

81 successors are appointed. A vacancy shall be filled in the

82 manner provide for the original appointment. An individual

83 appointed to fill a vacancy on the council shall serve only

84 for the remaining unexpired portion of the term.

                      Page 3
                HB365 Enrolled

85   (d) Each council member shall serve at the pleasure of

86 the appointing authority for a four-year renewable term.

87   (e) Members of the STEM Council shall serve without

88 compensation except for reimbursement for reasonable travel

89 and other expenses actually incurred as an incident to their

90 service on the council as approved by the Chair of the STEM

91 Council. Expenses shall be paid using any funds appropriated

92 for use by the council in the same manner as is provided by

93 law for state officers and employees.

94   Section 4. The STEM Council shall have the following

95 committees:

96   (1) The executive committee. The STEM Council shall be

97 under the direction of an executive committee, consisting of

98 the individuals identified in subdivisions (a)(1), (a)(2), and

99 (a)(4) of Section 3, and shall be led by the Chair of the STEM

100 Council, appointed by the Governor. The business of the

101 council shall be conducted by the executive committee, which

102 may launch initiatives, make final recommendations, approve

103 budgets, evaluate the director, and take any other action in

104 furtherance of the council's purpose and duties, consulting

105 with the other members of the council and the Secretary of

106 Workforce, as it deems appropriate.

107  (2) Additional committees. The chair of the council may

108 form additional committees to address issues concerning the

109 purpose and duties of the council. For each committee formed,

110 the chair shall designate a serving member of the council as

111 committee chair and shall provide for the membership and

112 duties of the committee. Members appointed to additional

                Page 4
                   HB365 Enrolled

113 committees may also be serving as members of the council.

114  Section 5. (a) Regular meetings of the council shall be

115 held at least annually and from time to time at the call of

116 the council chair. Committee meetings shall be held at the

117 call of the committee chair. Members of the council may

118 participate in a meeting of the council by means of telephone

119 conference, video conference, or similar communications

120 equipment by means of which all individuals participating in

121 the meeting may hear each other at the same time.

122 Participation by electronic means shall constitute presence in

123 person at a meeting for all purposes. The council shall

124 establish procedures and other policies necessary to perform

125 the business of the council, including the establishment of a

126 quorum.

127  (b) All regular and special meetings of the council,

128 including committee meetings, shall comply with the Open

129 Meetings Act.

130  Section 6. The duties of the Director of the STEM

131 Council, with guidance from the council and the Chief

132 Workforce Officer, shall include all of the following:

133  (1) Revise, as needed, and oversee the implementation

134 of the state STEM strategic plan, working in concert with the

135 Alabama Workforce Board's State Strategic Plan.

136  (2) Serve as the primary point of contact for

137 communicating with state and national STEM organizations.

138  (3) Coordinate communication efforts to increase STEM

139 awareness, illustrate the value of STEM learning, and

140 highlight STEM careers across the state.

                   Page 5
                    HB365 Enrolled

141  (4) Develop and implement the Governor's STEM "Seal of

142 Approval," using an evidence-based rubric to recognize

143 individual programs or periodic events that advance STEM

144 principles and learning across the state.

145  (5) Develop, implement, and maintain a digital registry

146 of evidence-based and effective STEM instructional and

147 professional learning strategies including summer,

148 after-school, and out-of-school programs, project-based lesson

149 plans, inquiry driven modules, and professional learning

150 opportunities.

151  (6) In collaboration with the Alabama Commission on the

152 Evaluation of Services, conduct comprehensive, external

153 evaluations, as needed or requested, of STEM-based curricula

154 and educational initiatives that receive funding from the

155 Education Trust Fund, beginning with the Alabama Math,

156 Science, and Technology Initiative; Technology in Motion;

157 Office of Math Improvement; and the Alabama Science in Motion

158 programs. Assessment rubrics should incorporate criteria

159 relevant to the goals and outcomes of each initiative

160 including, but not limited to, the impact on learning,

161 effectiveness of human capital and organizational performance,

162 cost per participant, fidelity of implementation, equitable

163 access, the potential for engendering interest and engagement

164 in STEM activities, and other criteria.

165  (7) Develop continuous improvement plans for the

166 Alabama Math, Science, and Technology Initiative; Technology

167 in Motion; Office of Math Improvement; and the Alabama Science

168 in Motion programs based on the data gathered from the

                    Page 6
                           HB365 Enrolled

169 external evaluations.

170  (8) In collaboration with the Alabama Commission on the

171 Evaluation of Services, conduct comprehensive evaluations, as

172 needed or requested, of state preservice preparation and

173 in-service programs for educators teaching STEM subjects,

174 including both traditional and alternate pathways.

175  (9) Expand opportunities for STEM career exploration

176 and discovery, especially among groups that are

177 under-represented in STEM professions.

178  (10) Collaborate with the Alabama Commission on Higher

179 Education, the State Board of Education, and the deans of

180 two-year and four-year institutions of higher education to

181 develop and enact an effective plan to encourage and support

182 an increase in the number of college students choosing a STEM

183 teaching career.

184  (11) In collaboration with the Alabama Commission on

185 the Evaluation of Services and the Office of Education and

186 Workforce Statistics, collect, aggregate, and analyze relevant

187 STEM data to track progress against the state STEM strategic

188 plan, including data from the Alabama Terminal on Linking and

189 Analyzing Statistics (ATLAS) on career pathways, state

190 agencies, including student STEM enrollment, learning, and

191 achievement data, STEM labor market information, and measures

192 of both STEM and STEM career awareness, attitudes, and

193 perceptions from learners across the education and workforce

194 continuum.

195  (12) Collaborate with business and industry in the

196 state to determine current and future STEM workforce needs.

                           Page 7
                            HB365 Enrolled

197  (13) Promote in-demand Alabama STEM careers, aligning

198 them with viable educational opportunities.

199  (14) Provide an annual report on progress toward

200 implementing the state STEM strategic plan, with

201 recommendations for improvement, to all of the following:

202  a. The Governor.

203  b. The Lieutenant Governor.

204  c. The Secretary of Workforce.

205  d. The State Workforce Board.

206  e. The Speaker of the House of Representatives.

207  f. The President Pro Tempore of the Senate.

208  g. The Chairs of the Senate Finance and Taxation

209 Education and House Ways and Means Education Committees.

210  h. The State Superintendent of Education.

211  i. The Chancellor of the Alabama Community College

212 System.

213  j. The Executive Director of the Alabama Commission on

214 Higher Education.

215  (15) Perform other tasks as requested by the Governor,

216 the Secretary of Workforce, and the Legislature.

217  Section 7. (a) The council shall receive

218 administrative, legal, and clerical support from the Chief of

219 the Workforce Pathways Division of the department.

220  (b) The department may retain the services of outside

221 experts, using funds appropriated for use by the council, on

222 behalf of the council.

223  (c) Upon request of the council, state agencies and

224 other state-funded entities shall cooperate with the council

                            Page 8
              HB365 Enrolled

225 and provide any requested information and data.

226  Section 8. The council may establish a foundation to

227 assist in the development and implementation of the programs

228 to promote STEM education, STEM workforce development, and

229 STEM careers. The foundation may solicit and receive

230 contributions from private organizations to accomplish

231 objectives provided in this act.

232  Section 9. This act shall become effective on October

233 1, 2025.
