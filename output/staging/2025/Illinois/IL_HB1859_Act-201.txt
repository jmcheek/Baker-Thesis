HB1859 Enrolled		LRB104 05178 LNS 15207 b

	1		    AN ACT concerning education.

	2		    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois,

	3		represented in the General Assembly:

	4		    Section 5. The Public Community College Act is amended by

	5		adding Section 3-29.28 as follows:

	6		    (110 ILCS 805/3-29.28 new)

	7		    Sec. 3-29.28. Use of artificial intelligence in

	8		instruction.

	9		    (a) As used in this Section, "artificial intelligence" has

	10		the same meaning given to the term in Section 2-101 of the

	11		Illinois Human Rights Act.

	12		    (b) Each board shall require the faculty member who

	13		teaches a course to be an individual who meets the

	14		qualifications set forth in 23 Ill. Adm. Code 1501.303(f) and

	15		any other applicable rules adopted by the State Board. A

	16		course may not, in lieu of a faculty member, use artificial

	17		intelligence as the sole source of instruction for students.

	18		    (c) Nothing in this Section shall be construed to prohibit

	19		a faculty member from using artificial intelligence to augment

	20		course instruction.
