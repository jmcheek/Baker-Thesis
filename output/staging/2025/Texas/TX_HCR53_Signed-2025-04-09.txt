H.C.R. No. 53

 	   	 HOUSE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION

 	   	 WHEREAS, The Canyon High School FFA Ag Issues team won first

 	   	place in the 2024 Texas FFA State Agricultural Issues Forum, held at

 	   	Sam Houston State University; and

 	   	 WHEREAS, The Agricultural Issues Forum Leadership

 	   	Development Event is a contest where students research a current

 	   	agricultural issue, create a portfolio, develop a presentation that

 	   	outlines the pros and cons of their topic, and present their

 	   	findings to a panel of judges; and

 	   	 WHEREAS, The Canyon High School FFA Ag Issues team of Presley

 	   	Davis, Lilli Klose, Gunnar McConlogue, Louden McConlogue, Evey

 	   	McIntosh, and Allison Wade delivered an exceptional presentation on

 	   	the use of artificial intelligence in agriculture; and

 	   	 WHEREAS, Through countless hours of preparation and an

 	   	unwavering commitment to excellence, the members of the Canyon High

 	   	School FFA Ag Issues team have brought tremendous credit to their

 	   	school and their community, and they may take justifiable pride in

 	   	their outstanding accomplishments; now, therefore, be it

 	   	 RESOLVED, That the 89th Legislature of the State of Texas

 	   	hereby congratulate the Canyon High School FFA Ag Issues team on

 	   	winning first place in the 2024 Texas FFA State Agricultural Issues

 	   	Forum and extend to all those associated with the team sincere best

 	   	wishes for continued success; and, be it further

 	   	 RESOLVED, That an official copy of this resolution be

 	   	prepared for the team as an expression of high regard by the Texas

 	   	House of Representatives and Senate.
