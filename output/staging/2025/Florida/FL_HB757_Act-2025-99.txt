33  Section 1. Section 800.045, Florida Statutes, is created

34 to read:

35  800.045 Lewd or lascivious images; penalties.—

36  (1) As used in this section, the term:

37  (a) "Identifiable minor" means a person:

38  1. Who was less than 16 years of age at the time the image

39 was created, altered, adapted, or modified, or whose image as a

40 person less than 16 years of age was used in the creating,

41 altering, adapting, or modifying of the image; and

42  2. Who is recognizable as an actual person by the person's

43 face, likeness, or other distinguishing characteristic, such as

44 a unique birthmark, or other recognizable feature.

45

46 The term may not be construed to require proof of the actual

47 identity of the identifiable minor.

48  (b) "Intentionally view" has the same meaning as in s.

49 827.071.
